3EXT. ALLEY — DAY

Super: II Flatiron Health 2023 – winter 2025

An empty concrete alley between a block wall and a building. Hard sun, blue sky.

5EXT. SIDEWALK — DUSK

Super: IV Munich summer 2022

Dusk. A brick wall on a sidewalk, lit red by a car's taillight.

Gabe (V.O.)

in summer 2022 i did research on self-driving cars at the technical university of munich.

i translated traffic trajectories from drone footage of german intersections

into semantic scenarios for autonomous-vehicle testing, and improved the evaluation loop.

the write-up is here: read the paper.

it was incredible to spend time living in germany.
