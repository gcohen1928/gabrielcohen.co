Profile 23

Now

Most of Gabe Cohen’s time goes to Nero, where he has been CTO since the spring of 2025. The company helps artists make money and go viral on livestreams. He built nero.fan and scaled it to more than 600,000 monthly users; by early 2026 it was processing upwards of $500,000 in GMV a month.

“This is where most
of my time is spent.”

On Nero

Before

Before that he was a software engineer at Flatiron Health, first as an intern in 2023, then from the summer of 2024 to the winter of 2025. He shipped clinical research products that supported clinical trials, and built infrastructure and features for a full-stack patient management system. Eight months in, he had his own team, building internal sales tools. He also led AI development initiatives across Flatiron.

College

In college he built web and mobile apps for Daedalus Finance, a fintech startup, and for Feniska, a pet-tech startup in Germany. He also, in his words, “started a few failed consumer apps.”

24 Gabriel Cohen

Munich, 2022

In the summer of 2022 he did research on self-driving cars at the Technical University of Munich. He translated traffic trajectories, taken from drone footage of German intersections, into semantic scenarios for testing autonomous vehicles, and improved the evaluation loop. The write-up, Automatically Derived Semantic Scenario Instance Descriptions, is on this site. “It was incredible to spend time living in Germany,” he says.

Outside work

He is learning to play guitar, reading, and doing “other shit that caught my attention”: right now, skiing and photography. The guitar is a yellow Stratocaster (p. 28). The reading leans to fiction (p. 29). The photographs are the ones in this feature.
