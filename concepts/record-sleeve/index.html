notes

by gabe,
san francisco

hey, i’m gabe. i’m 23 and i live in san francisco.

A1i’m the CTO of nero. we’re helping artists make money and go viral on livestreams. i built nero.fan and scaled it to 600K+ monthly users, and it processes $500K+ in GMV a month (as of early 2026). i’ve been there since spring 2025, and this is where most of my time is spent.

A2before that i was a software engineer at flatiron health, from summer 2024 to winter 2025, after interning there in 2023. i shipped clinical research products supporting clinical trials, and built infrastructure and features for a full-stack patient management system. after 8 months i got my own team, building internal sales tools, and i led AI development initiatives across flatiron.

A3 A4 A6in college i built web and mobile apps for daedalus finance, a fintech startup, and feniska, a german pet-tech startup. i also started a few failed consumer apps.

A5in the summer of 2022 i did research at the technical university of munich on self-driving cars: translating traffic trajectories from drone footage of german intersections into semantic scenarios for autonomous-vehicle testing, and improving the evaluation loop. the paper write-up is here. it was incredible to spend time living in germany.

Boutside of work i’m learning how to play guitar, reading, and doing other shit that caught my attention. right now that’s skiing and photography.
